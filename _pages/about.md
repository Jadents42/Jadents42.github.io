I'm a mechatronics engineer finishing my BS at Texas A&M in December 2026, with a minor in Embedded Systems. I work on robots that manipulate and move through the physical world — arms, hands, and mobile systems — rather than ones that just drive or fly.

Right now I'm a research technician at the Center for Infrastructure Renewal, where I got a dual-arm mobile manipulator running autonomous pipeline inspection from the ground up — full coordinated control of both 6-DOF arms, the grippers, and the mobile base, plus a Gazebo simulation I'm extending toward autonomous corrosion detection. I'm also building a custom pipe inspection crawler, and I lead the sensors team on a $1.2M NASA-funded coastal monitoring capstone.

Outside of research, I built a 3-DOF robotic arm from scratch — deriving the kinematics by hand and writing my own motion planning — because I wanted to understand the math underneath the tools, not just use them.

I'm applying to graduate school for Spring 2027 to keep going deeper into manipulation and robotic mechanism design. Take a look at my [portfolio](/portfolio/) for the details, or my [resume](/files/resume.pdf) for the short version.
